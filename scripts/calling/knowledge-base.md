## What is Nexorra?

Nexorra is an AI-powered appointment-setting agency for real estate agents in the US and Canada.
We handle the entire outbound prospecting process so agents can focus exclusively on closing deals.

**The problem we solve**: Real estate agents spend 60-70% of their time on lead generation and
follow-up — time that should be spent with qualified buyers and sellers.

**What we do**: We scrape and qualify real estate leads, run targeted cold email + calling + Instagram
outreach campaigns, and book discovery calls directly into agents' calendars. The agent shows up to
the call and closes.

## How It Works (the process)

1. **Lead Sourcing**: We scrape agent contact data from major brokerages (eXp, RE/MAX, Century 21,
   Sotheby's, Compass, BHHS) across all US timezone markets
2. **Multi-Channel Outreach**: Cold email (via Instantly), Instagram DM sequences, and AI phone calls
3. **Personalization**: Every touchpoint is personalized — we research the agent's background, market,
   and brokerage before reaching out
4. **Booking**: When an agent shows interest, we book them directly into a Calendly calendar
5. **Handoff**: The Nexorra team shows up to the discovery call with the agent's full context

## Common Objections & Responses

**"I already have enough leads / I'm too busy"**
That's actually the best time to start — we set up the system while you're busy so when things
slow down (and they always do) you have a full pipeline. Takes 20 mins to onboard.

**"I tried this before and it didn't work"**
What we do is different — we don't send generic blasts. Every outreach is personalized to the agent's
specific market and brokerage. Also, we use AI to qualify interest before booking, so you're only
talking to people who genuinely want to learn more.

**"How is this different from other lead gen services?"**
Most services give you leads and leave you to follow up yourself. We do the entire outreach and booking
process — you just show up to the calls. Also, we focus specifically on real estate agents as leads,
not just buyer/seller leads.

**"I need to think about it / talk to my partner"**
Totally understand. What would help you make the decision? Happy to send a one-pager and a recording
of what the outreach looks like in practice.

**"What's the commitment / can I cancel?"**
We have month-to-month options. Most clients stay because they're seeing results, not because they're
locked in.

**"How long until I see results?"**
Most agents see their first booked call within the first week. GCI results typically show up in month 2
as those initial calls convert to deals.
