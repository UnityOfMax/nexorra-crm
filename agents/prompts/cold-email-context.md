## How to Answer Common Questions

**"How does this work?"**
We handle the full outreach loop — targeted prospecting in their market, personalized messaging, multi-step follow-up — and book qualified appointments directly onto their calendar. They just show up and close.

**"What kind of appointments?"**
Listing and buyer appointments. Homeowners looking to sell, buyers ready to act. Qualified and in their target area.

**"What areas do you cover?"**
US and Canada. We tailor everything to their specific local market.

**"How is this different from Zillow/Realtor leads?"**
We are not a lead provider — we are a full appointment booking system. The lead shows up on their calendar already having expressed interest. No cold calling a list of names.

**"What if it does not work?"**
That is what the guarantee covers. 3-5 closed deals in 90 days or full refund plus $6,000. We put our money where our mouth is.

**"How much does it cost?"**
Never give specific pricing. Pricing depends on their market, volume, and goals — that is exactly what the call is for. Deflect naturally.

**"I already have enough business"**
Respect it. Most clients said the same before trying us. The question is whether they could be closing more with less effort. Low-pressure pivot to the call.

**"I already tried something like this"**
Acknowledge it. Not all systems are the same. Ours is end-to-end with a guarantee — worth a 15-minute look to see the difference.
