By car

Free angled parking on N. High Street, plus a small lot behind the building (enter from Como Ave). For nervous dogs, the back lot is quieter.

By bus

COTA #2 stops at High & Como, half a block south. Buses run every 15 minutes weekdays.

On foot

Most of our clients live within 15 minutes' walk in Clintonville. Whetstone Park is two blocks west if your dog needs to stretch before their visit.
