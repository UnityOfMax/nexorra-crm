Home/About

A grooming studio
on a quiet block.

Three groomers, one room, six appointments a day. We opened in a converted bakery in Clintonville in 2017 and we've kept it small on purpose.

01 — The Story

We started because we
couldn't find it ourselves.

In 2016, Mara was driving her arthritic Cocker, Bramble, across town to four different salons trying to find one that wouldn't crate him for three hours between bath and dryer. She didn't.

The Wagsworth idea was simple and stubborn: one dog in the room at a time, no kennel time, no time pressure on the groomer. The first appointment was Bramble, on October 4, 2017. The schedule has been booked since week six.

Nine years on, the room hasn't changed much. We added a second table when we hired Jordan in 2021, then a third when we hired Theo in 2024. We still cap the day at six dogs.

02 — What we believe

Four rules
we won't break.

We're not the cheapest groomer in Columbus and we're not trying to be. These are the things we won't compromise on — they're the whole reason we exist.

Rule 01

One dog at a time

Your dog is the only one in the room. No barking neighbors, no waiting in a kennel, no over-handling between steps.

Rule 02

Never a cage dryer

Every dog gets a full hand-held high-velocity dry. It takes longer. It's worth it.

Rule 03

No behavior surcharges

If your dog needs more time, we book more time. We won't charge you because your dog had a hard day.

Rule 04

Skip the step

If today isn't the day for nails, ears, or the full cut, we stop. A no-charge re-do beats a bad memory.
