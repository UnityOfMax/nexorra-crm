01 — The Studio

Small studio.
Big difference.

We opened Wagsworth in a converted bakery on N. High Street because the grooming we wanted for our own dogs didn't exist in Columbus. Quiet rooms. One dog at a time. Real conversations about coat, skin, and behavior — not assembly-line haircuts.

Read our story →

Founder's note

"I wanted a place where my own anxious mutt could relax. So we built one."

Before Wagsworth, I groomed in busy salons for nine years. I watched good dogs get over-stimulated, good groomers get rushed, and good coats get clipped when they should have been scissored. So we set up a single grooming table, hired three groomers I trust with my own dogs, and capped the schedule at six appointments a day.

It's slower. It's quieter. And it's the only way we want to do this.

Mara Whitfield  Owner & Lead Groomer

03 — How a visit goes

Four hours,
tops.

Most dogs are in and out in two to three hours. We text you a "ready" photo the moment we're finishing up — no waiting around, no kennel time.

Step 01

Hands-on check

We feel through the coat, check ears, paws, and skin, and confirm the cut we agreed on at booking.

Step 02

Bath & blow-out

Two-step bath, plant-based shampoo matched to coat and skin. A full high-velocity dry — no cage dryers, ever.

Step 03

Cut & finish

Hand-scissor work for fluffies, clipper-and-blend for shorter coats. Nails, ears, sanitary, and pads to finish.

Step 04

Ready for pickup

You get a "ready" text with a portrait, notes about the coat, and a recommended return window.

Inside the room

Calm tools.
Calmer dogs.

Quiet clippers (~62 dB), a low-velocity finishing dryer, non-slip hydraulic table, and the same three groomers every week. Your dog meets the same human, the same room, the same routine — every time.

We're Fear-Free Certified, which means we'd rather skip a step than fight a dog through it. If today isn't the day for nails, we'll book a no-charge follow-up and try again.
