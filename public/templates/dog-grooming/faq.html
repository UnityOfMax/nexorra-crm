01 — Booking & pricing
Two to three weeks for standard appointments, four to six weeks if you want a specific groomer or a Saturday. If you book a standing appointment we'll automatically hold your slot a full year out.
Final cost depends on size, coat condition, and the cut you want. Once you tell us a little about your dog, we'll confirm a flat number when we book you. That flat number is what you pay — no day-of surcharges.
No. If your dog needs more time, we book more time at the same rate. If a step has to be skipped (a nail trim, say) we'll bring you back for a no-charge follow-up. We will never charge you because your dog had a hard day.
All major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and good old cash. We don't take checks. Tips are appreciated but never expected and 100% of any tip goes to the groomer who worked on your dog.
Free to cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours out. Inside 24 hours, we ask for a 25% deposit before booking your next appointment. Same-day reschedules due to illness are always free — just text us.
02 — The visit
Most dogs are in and out in 2–3 hours. Full grooms on big or heavily-coated dogs can run a little longer. We always text you a "ready" photo when we're finishing up — no waiting in a kennel, no surprise calls.
For first visits with anxious dogs, yes — we'll set up a chair in the corner. Otherwise, most dogs relax faster without their humans in the room. We're happy to send photos and video updates throughout the visit if you'd like.
Tell us at booking. For reactive dogs we'll schedule a "first/last" slot so the studio is empty when you arrive and when you leave. For anxious dogs we may suggest two or three short intro visits (no charge) before the first full groom. We're Fear-Free Certified and we mean it.
No. We love cats, but we don't have the right setup for them. For cats in Columbus we recommend Whisker & Comb on Oakland Park — Mara's cat goes there.
We'll do our best to brush it out, but for the dog's comfort there's a point where a shorter cut is the kinder choice. We'll always show you the matting and talk through options before we make a decision — and we'll send you home with a brushing plan so it doesn't happen again.
03 — Coat, skin & care
Depends on the coat. Most fluffies: every 4–6 weeks. Short-coated dogs: every 8–10 weeks for a bath. Double-coats: a de-shed every 6–8 weeks in shedding season, less often otherwise. We'll always recommend a return window at pickup based on what we see.
We carry three plant-based, sulfate-free lines. We pick one based on your dog's coat and skin: a deep-clean for greasy double-coats, a soothing oatmeal-and-aloe for sensitive skin, and a hydrating shea blend for dry coats. Everything is dye-free and lightly scented or fragrance-free.
Often, yes. If your vet has recommended a medicated bath we're happy to use whatever they've prescribed. For garden-variety dry/itchy skin we can recommend a coat-soothing soak as an add-on. We're not vets — for anything serious we'll always defer to your dog's care team.
Both. Jordan in particular trained in show-grooming and can do most breed-standard cuts (Schnauzers, Poodles, Westies, Scotties, terriers in general). For pet trims we'll honor whatever style you like — bring a photo if you have one.
04 — Health & safety
Yes — Rabies and DHPP, both current. Bordetella is strongly recommended but not required (we're a one-dog-at-a-time studio, so the risk is low). Please email a copy of records to hi@wagsworth.studio before your first visit.
We'll text you a photo and a note. Most often it's nothing scary — a lump, a hot spot, a chipped tooth, an ear that needs a vet look. We document everything in your dog's file and bring it up next visit if it persists.
Free angled parking in front of the studio on N. High Street, plus a small lot behind the building (enter from Como Ave). For drop-off, we recommend the back lot — it's quieter for nervous dogs.
